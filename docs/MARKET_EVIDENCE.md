# Market evidence and open questions

This evidence describes payment-system context. It does not prove demand, whitespace, willingness to pay, or a benefit from this demonstrator. Checked 18 November 2026.

| Public source | Supported observation | Scope and caveat |
|---|---|---|
| [CPMI cross-border payments programme](https://www.bis.org/committees/cpmi/cross-border-payments/overview) | Interoperability, operating hours, access policies and data exchange are active cross-border payment constraints; aligning operating hours can support liquidity management. | Policy/system overview, not a buyer study or proof that a separate planning product is needed. |
| [2026 cross-border payments monitoring brief](https://www.bis.org/publications/cpmi-brief-13-enhancing-cross-border-payments-step-step-insights-2025-monitoring-survey) | A survey of 82 jurisdictions reports varied progress on payment-system interoperability, hours, data standards and access. | Jurisdiction-level monitoring; not representative evidence of customer pain or product purchase intent. |
| [Payment-system interlinking framework](https://www.bis.org/publications/interlinking-payment-systems-and-role-application-programming-interfaces-framework-cross-border-payments) | Interlinking can shorten transaction chains, with design challenges across governance, operating standards, risks and implementation. | Framework analysis, not proof of a particular platform’s performance. |
| [Financial Stability Board data interoperability recommendations](https://www.fsb.org/2024/12/recommendations-to-promote-alignment-and-interoperability-across-data-frameworks-related-to-cross-border-payments-final-report/) | Cross-border payment data rules affect processing, privacy, risk controls and information exchange. | Regulatory recommendations; actual requirements vary by jurisdiction and use case. |

## What is established and what remains open

**Established by these sources:** cross-border processing spans systems with different access, operating, data and governance constraints.

**To validate:** whether payment operators experience material conflicts across shared cash and route limits; whether existing orchestration and treasury systems already resolve them; whether historical records can support fair replay; and whether any measurable improvement warrants a separate purchase.

No private buyer data or company-specific market claims are included. No customer research, adoption, savings or operational improvement is claimed.
