## Established vs. to validate

**Established:** cross-border cost, FX transparency and reconciliation challenges are reported; payment operations span multiple system boundaries; agent-mediated B2B payment activity is being piloted.

**To validate:** whether operators need a new portfolio-aware execution layer beyond current payment orchestration and treasury tooling; whether they will share route, balance and outcome data; whether reservation and scheduling decisions measurably improve outcomes; and whether a dedicated vendor can be purchased and integrated.
