## Excluded or not counted

- Expired treasury/payment solicitations were not counted as current opportunities, even if they indicate historical category spend.
- Generic ecosystem, innovation, award and accelerator announcements were excluded because they do not show a concrete buyer requirement or budget route.
- A public tender is a category-demand signal, not evidence that the procuring organization needs a cross-provider optimizer or that this repository is qualified to bid.

## Interpretation

This search found **one** current, verifiable public procurement signal with a strong adjacent treasury fit. It supports continued investigation of TMS/liquidity buyer workflows but does not establish a standalone market, a named lead for this product, or a viable sales route. The next demand evidence should come from operators who can confirm a recurring constrained-allocation incident and provide a permissioned replay dataset.
